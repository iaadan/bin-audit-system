R.O.B.O.T.S. - Six Standards for Perfect Bins

R

Right Size Bin

Items must fit appropriately in the bin size. Match item size to bin capacity to prevent damage and improve accessibility.

  • Proper sizing for items
  • Prevents damage
  • Easier location retrieval
O

Overhang

No items should hang over the bin edge. Prevents items from falling and maintains clear aisle access.

  • Prevents item falling
  • Reduces safety hazards
  • Maintains clear aisles
B

Blocking

Items should not block bin labels or barcodes. Ensures quick scanning and reduces pick time.

  • Labels clearly visible
  • Reduces pick time
  • Prevents wrong selections
O

Overstuffed

Bins should not be packed beyond capacity. Maintains item integrity and allows easy removal.

  • Maintains integrity
  • Prevents damage
  • Easy item removal
T

Titles Out

Product labels/titles should face outward. Enables quick visual identification and reduces search time.

  • Quick identification
  • Reduces search time
  • Improves accuracy
S

Same ASIN

Only identical items (same ASIN) in each bin. Critical for preventing wrong item picks.

  • Eliminates confusion
  • Prevents wrong picks
  • Critical accuracy
